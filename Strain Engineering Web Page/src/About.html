About the company

Engineering with integrity and a long view.

Strain Engineering (Pvt) Ltd is a Zimbabwean multidisciplinary company registered in 2013 to deliver engineering, environmental and management services.

Our background

Built around water, infrastructure and sustainable resource management.

Strain Engineering operates across civil, structural, water and environmental engineering, alongside corporate governance, strategic management and business contract management consultancy. Our work is informed by integrated water and natural-resource management, sustainability and environmental justice.

Our core team is complemented by local, regional and international professional associates with expertise in engineering design, environmental science, analytical chemistry, surveying, GIS, information technology and biological sciences.

“To be the centre of excellence in service provision of a well-built and well-managed environment.”
