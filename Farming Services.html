Glen Brook Vines Agro-Services

Productive farming rooted in practical water management.

Strain Engineering’s agro-production activity focuses on commercial horticulture and crop production at Glen Brook Farm, Nyabira.

Our approach

Commercial crops, supported by appropriate irrigation.

Glen Brook Farm is located in Nyabira, approximately 25 km from Harare on the Harare-Chirundu Road. The farm’s crop-production work is shaped by irrigation, water-storage and energy planning suited to local conditions.

We grow a diverse range of horticultural, commercial and seed crops, applying engineering and water-management capability to farm operations.

Crop production

A diverse portfolio for commercial markets.

Horticulture

Vegetables and fruit crops including tomatoes, potatoes, chillies, peppers, paprika, groundnuts, watermelons and avocados.

Field crops

Commercial and seed maize, soya bean and other seasonal crop-production activities.

Farm systems

Drip irrigation, water storage and careful resource use support reliable, quality production.
