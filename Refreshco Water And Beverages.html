Refreshco Water & Beverages

Quality-focused water and beverage solutions.

Refreshco is a Strain Engineering business unit focused on bulk water, bottled water and beverage production.

Our products

Clean water, prepared with care.

Refreshco produces mineral water and a range of beverages. Treatment processes include reverse osmosis and UV treatment to support quality-focused production.

We offer bottled products as well as bulk-water supply, providing a practical service for household, retail and commercial requirements.
